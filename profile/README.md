# Arcane Tech

**Practical web development, hosting, and technical solutions without the nonsense.**

Arcane Tech is an independent web and technology business focused on building reliable, maintainable websites and infrastructure for small businesses, organizations, and people who would rather spend their time running their business than fighting with technology.

This GitHub organization is home to the tools, integrations, experiments, and open source projects behind Arcane Tech.

## What We Do

Arcane Tech primarily works with:

- **WordPress development** and custom site solutions
- **Website hosting and infrastructure**
- **Performance, security, and maintenance**
- **Automation and integrations**
- **Custom development and internal tooling**
- **Technical troubleshooting and consulting**

The goal is simple: build technology that works, stays understandable, and does not become somebody else's nightmare six months later.

## Our Approach

Good technology does not need to be complicated for the sake of being complicated.

We favor:

- Simple, maintainable solutions
- Sensible automation
- Reliable infrastructure
- Clear documentation
- Security by default
- Ownership and portability
- Tools that solve actual problems

If a solution needs twelve services, three dashboards, and a blood sacrifice just to publish a website, something has probably gone wrong.

## Infrastructure

Arcane Tech's hosting and operations stack includes technologies such as:

`WordPress` · `PHP` · `MariaDB` · `Linux` · `Cloudflare` · `Docker` · `GitHub` · `Vultr` · `Hetzner`

We also build and maintain internal tooling for deployment, monitoring, backups, automation, and client site management.

## About Arcane Tech

Arcane Tech is built around a straightforward philosophy:

> Technology should make your work easier, not give you another job.

That means avoiding unnecessary complexity, choosing tools based on the problem instead of the hype cycle, and building systems that can actually be understood and maintained.
